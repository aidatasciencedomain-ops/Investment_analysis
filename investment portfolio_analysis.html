JPM Stock Price Monte Carlo Forecast (Geometric Brownian Motion)

Stochastic Engine

10 independent price trajectories generated using continuous Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM): $S_t = S_{t-1} \cdot \exp\left((\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2)\Delta t + \sigma \sqrt{\Delta t} Z\right)$, with empirical drift $\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 = 0.000431$ and daily volatility $\sigma = 0.019134$.
